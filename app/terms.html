OnCall is open-source software under the MIT License. These terms cover use of a self-hosted OnCall server and its Alexa+ add-on.

What OnCall does

OnCall reads your metrics, deployments and logs, proposes a rollback for the release that lines up with an incident, and runs it only after you approve that exact action. Approval takes an explicit confirmation of the action ID, or a one-time code sent to your pager and spoken back within 90 seconds. Saying "yes" alone never changes production.

Your responsibilities

  • Connect OnCall only to systems you own or are authorized to operate.
  • Keep the operator key, OAuth client secret and paging channel private. Anyone with them can act as the operator.
  • Review each proposed action before approving it. You are responsible for the changes you approve.
